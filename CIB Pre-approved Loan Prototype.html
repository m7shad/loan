1. Parties

Commercial International Bank (Egypt) S.A.E. ("the Bank") and the customer identified in the CIB mobile app ("the Borrower").

2. Facility

The Bank grants the Borrower an unsecured personal loan for the amount and tenor shown in the loan summary, subject to the pre-approved limit.

3. Profit rate

A fixed flat rate of 22% per annum is applied on the original principal for the full tenor. Total repayment equals principal plus flat profit.

4. Repayment

Equal monthly installments are automatically deducted from the linked account on the same day each month, starting one month after disbursement.

5. Early settlement

The Borrower may settle the loan early in full. Early settlement is subject to the Central Bank of Egypt regulations in force at the time of settlement.

6. Late payment

If an installment is not paid on its due date, the Bank may charge late-payment fees permitted by CBE rules and may report the delay to the credit bureau (I-Score).

Administrative fees are waived for this pre-approved offer. Stamp duty, where applicable, is borne by the Borrower.

The Borrower authorizes the Bank to verify information and access credit data as required for this facility.
